Solid to SCAD

STL files in, one parametric .scad out, ready for MakerWorld's Parametric Model Maker.

1. Parts

    The first part is the reference: the sizes describe its outer dimensions.

    2. Sizes

    Each size stretches the part between its two cut planes and moves the rest without changing shape. On the strip, green means a clean cut: drag the handles to move the planes. Double-click the view to re-frame it.

    3. Export

    Add one or more STL files to start.
